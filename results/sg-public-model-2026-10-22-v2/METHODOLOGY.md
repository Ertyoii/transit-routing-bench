# Methodology and mandatory limits

## Finite research question and original design

Compare quality, ordinary response clocks, process availability, preparation and sampled memory of 18 specifically named routing service products on one frozen public modeled transit network. All 18 products remain in the final descriptive tables. Validation consumed a finite predeclared budget; remaining gains at exhaustion are not a plateau, proof of optimality or permission for final-driven tuning. There were no new optimizers, routing/scoring methods, algorithm changes, searches or measurement reruns in the construction of this release.

The primary point-request scope is stop endpoints in four weekday windows, a one-hour arrival horizon, at most four boardings and at most 600 seconds in each access, egress, transfer or direct-walk role, with at most 3,000 seconds total walking and the frozen transfer semantics. Frequency phases and OSM-derived walking topology are model assumptions, not verified physical access or observed departures. This is a public community model, not latest official LTA data or physical truth. Only 431 of 1,320 passenger-access offers are representable; the other 889 remain unsupported. Optional phase-B, rail and passenger robustness tiers are not final-routed results in this release.

The original V2 split used 240 tune, 360 validation and 720 test request records with canonical parent-pair separation from historical exposed data. The final records are not asserted to be spatially distinct physical OD pairs. Geographic and timetable-construction-positive halves remain original offers; construction metadata itself encodes positive feasibility information. Mirrored blocks repeat requests, not independent demand.

## Literal protocol deviation: outcome-sealed and input-aware

Original TUNE_PROTOCOL line 15 states: “Test bytes are hash-committed before routing, inaccessible by workflow until a separately hash-bound accepted finalist seal. V1 exposed test cannot become untouched by renaming or rehashing. Any accidental V2 exposure invalidates untouched status and requires a genuinely new split, with the exposure retained.”

Input-only access occurred before finalist sealing: all-split integrity/representation QA, registry JSON validation and separately declared native-domain/passenger auxiliary construction accessed heldout request inputs and construction metadata. Continuing the original split without a fresh split violates both the literal input-inaccessibility promise and its fresh-split clause. The original promise is not retroactively rewritten, and a new hash or label does not repair untouched status.

A pre-outcome continuation addendum explicitly retained this deviation and the original request seals. Logged primary source/selector/runtime/cap selection used tune outcomes; no validation/test routing or scalar-reference outcomes were evidenced as inputs to that selection. This is evidence-bounded absence, not proof against unlogged actions or proof of zero leakage. Available endpoint/constraint/cohort/construction metadata and query-conditioned auxiliary engineering can influence engineering selection; the magnitude of this input-aware influence is unquantified. Accordingly the final is outcome-sealed, input-aware evaluation, not a clean untouched-input or wholly inductive holdout.

Exact final release/source/configuration/selector/preference/resource/order bindings and both balanced blocks were sealed before final outcomes. There was no final-outcome-driven product selection, source change, filtering, resampling or replacement run. All unknown, unsupported, refusal and failure states stay visible. The eight presealed contiguous 18-process chunks preserve the global 144-process order and block reversal; archival gaps between chunks are disclosed, not erased or counted as native clock time.

## Independent checks, reference domains and denominators

The accepted final comprises 144 original processes and 25,920 offered ordinary observations. Of these, 25,912 are independently complete and eight are label-limited. No native imports are used. Independent finite acceptance covers 77,760 raw/final/display stage rows, 139,968 scalar comparisons and 2,880 typed candidate-contract rows with zero observed reconstruction errors and native counterexamples. Zero within these checks is not a universal correctness theorem.

Scalar truth is 1,296 distinct slots counted once across windows: 881 finite-found, 351 complete model-empty and 64 UNKNOWN. The nine frozen scalar weights do not prove a complete three-dimensional or continuous frontier. Scalar model-empty, unknown, missing/partial candidates and empty/unavailable contributor pools are distinct.

Each product's full positive-pool denominator is 490 original request records repeated twice times 393 preferences, or 385,140 comparisons. The other 230 original records repeated twice are 460 empty/unavailable-pool offers, not 393 successes per offer. Missing offers remain in the offered denominator; zero eligible pool comparisons mean null quality, not perfect quality.

The full observed union, leave-family-out and conservative leave-lineage-out pools remain incomplete. Raw candidate discovery, independently feasible retained final candidates and displayed subsets are separate stages. Maximum additive score-second regret and maximum normalized regret are SIGNED statistics, including in leave-out panels, not absolute-value regret or physical passenger delay. Negative excluded-pool regret indicates reference incompleteness, not better-than-global routing. Agreement is finite and numerical/tolerance-qualified, not global optimality, full 3D enumeration or real demand success.

## Product comparisons are not isolated causes

Keep native 2D/3D guarantees, algorithm/search order, source, requested k, label caps, GC, memory target and preference configuration distinct. p03 is old ORDINARY Mc500k; p04 is Source-B EARLY Mc500k. Their final contrast is not an isolated old-Early to Source-B-Early source optimization. The separate 5.2%/7.5% isolated old-Early to Source-B-Early improvements belong only to exposed tune evidence, not this final contrast.

p09–p15 share Source-B EARLY Mc500k, GOGC=50 and GOMEMLIMIT=4GiB with preference configuration e902…; p04 uses GOGC=100 and a different e15… preference configuration. Cross-cohort differences are not solely selector or source effects. k3 versus k5 changes cardinality and can change policy; equal aggregate quality does not establish identical outputs. Native ULTRA versus Mc, native TripBased versus walking-tie refinement and cap250k/500k/1m products are retained separately, without a universal winner. CSA families receive no new execution, optimization, donation or measurement in this V2 release; unchanged baseline dependencies are not new CSA experiments. CoreCH is graph-only and OTP is absent.

## Clocks, preparation and availability

Ordinary publication clocks measure local serialized-answer receipt for individual offered requests; empirical pooled nearest-rank quantiles use actual observations, including failure offers, rather than process-quantile averages or success-only denominators. Per-window/block p95 values remain exact cells. Across-four-window per-block p95 was not retained and is not fabricated by averaging cells.

A new serving process is launched for each ordered product/window/block workload. Only the first request is process-cold. Per-child spawn-to-first-native availability includes in-child provenance/input work, preparation, first computation and local publication overhead. First native TRANSIT (36 processes) and VALID-EMPTY (108 processes) are separate cohorts. Do not rename either cohort time-to-first-transit for all processes.

Filesystem pages are hash-warmed. Roughly 19–22 minutes per stage of source/input/model verification overhead outside the process clocks is separate from roughly 165–211 seconds of aggregate native-driver clocks. Neither is clean cold-disk startup, UI/network latency, production readiness, fsync durability or a service SLA. Router, checker and quality preparation; startup phase sums; first-native availability; ordinary response clocks; and process wall clocks are different distributions. Copied preparation fields are not a fresh independent preparation measurement. Sampled RSS and admission guards do not guarantee peak confinement or whole-campaign resource fit; a Go soft memory target is not a hard limit.
